One line, in the voice of a hardware wallet that will not sign a transaction at 3 AM.

> 3 AM you is not on my approved list. Come back when 9 AM you can vouch for him.

The wallet treats its owner as two different people: 3 AM you is not on the
approved list, and only 9 AM you can vouch for him.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (79 chars, single line, no trailing newline)
```
